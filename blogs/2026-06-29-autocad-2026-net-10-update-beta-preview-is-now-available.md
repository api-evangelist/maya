The AutoCAD 2026 .NET 10 Update Beta Preview is now available for developers through the Autodesk Feedback Portal. This preview introduces support for .NET 10 in AutoCAD 2026. This is a breaking update delivered during the release cycle, which is not something we have done in the past.
