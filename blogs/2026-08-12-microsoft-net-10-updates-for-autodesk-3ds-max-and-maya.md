Microsoft has announced that support for .NET 8 and .NET 9 will end on November 10, 2026. After this date, Microsoft will no longer provide servicing updates, security updates, or technical support for these versions. Many Autodesk products include components or dependencies that rely on Microsoft .NET technologies.
